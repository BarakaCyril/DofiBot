1. Coder

Writes and maintains the bot.

Step 1: Sets up the Node.js project in the repo (package.json, folder layout, a README explaining how to run it).
Steps 4–8: Builds each feature one at a time: join/leave, #silent-seat speech, /myvoice, live transcription, /catchup and /recap, then /pause, /resume and automatic deletion.
After each step, pushes working code to GitHub and tells the tester what to check.
Step 9: Fixes the problems the tester reports.
Step 10: Sets the bot up on the VPS with pm2, together with the accounts person.
Time: the most of any role, about one to two weekend days a week for roughly a month.
2. Tester

Checks that the bot works for the group, not just on the coder’s laptop.

Step 3 (the most important job): Gets everyone’s consent, records 10 minutes of a normal call, and uploads it to ElevenLabs Scribe on the website. Then goes through the transcript with the group and reports whether the Sheng/Kiswahili mix is usable.
Steps 4–8: After each step, tests it in the test server. For example: does the voice sound right, do names come out right, do transcripts show the correct speaker, does /pause really stop transcription?
Reports problems as GitHub issues, saying what they did, what they expected, and what happened.
Step 9: Runs two or three real test calls with the whole group, collects complaints, and ranks the top few for the coder.
Privacy checks: confirms transcripts are deleted after the recap.
3. Accounts & money

Owns the accounts, keys, and bills.

Step 2:
Creates the Discord test server.
Registers the bot in the Discord Developer Portal and invites it to the server.
Creates the ElevenLabs and Anthropic accounts and their API keys.
Sets a monthly spending limit on each paid service.
Gives keys to the coder privately (in person or through a password manager, never in the group chat). If a key leaks, replaces it with a new one straight away.
Checks spending each month and agrees with the group how costs are split.
Step 10: Rents and pays for the VPS.
Manages who has access to the GitHub repo (currently BarakaCyril’s account).
Decisions for the whole group

These aren’t anyone’s job alone. Agree on them before the first real call:

Consent to recording and transcription.
How long transcripts are kept (deleted right after the recap, or after something like 7 days).
What language summaries should be written in.
